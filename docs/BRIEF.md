# Implemented brief

A review prototype for Vivienne Lee and creative director Youssef. First review: 14 September 2026; project deadline: 25 September 2026.

## Direction

A clearer perspective: architectural calm, editorial scale and a financial partnership expressed through connected viewpoints rather than dashboards or invented performance figures. Preserve the approved navy/brass monogram and exact spelling **Lee Monarc**.

## Scope

Homepage holds the full narrative: proposition, four expandable areas of expertise, the perspective interaction, Vivienne introduction and contact invitation. Contact is its own practical page. No thin About/Services duplicates and no speculative brand deck.

## Content status

Editorial copy is provisional and requires client review. Business contact fields come from the supplied business card and HTML source. No client logos, testimonials, regulatory badges, promises of financial results or invented biography. e-CFO positioning comes from the project brief.

## Reference

Paraform inspected live in local Chromium: strong serif/sans contrast, confident whitespace, clear chapters, one dominant visual idea per section and user-controlled motion. We do not reuse its layout, content, media or identity.

## Acceptance

A working local production build; desktop/tablet/mobile layouts; usable keyboard navigation; reduced-motion and no-JavaScript fallbacks; exact supplied logos; documented edit points. This is a private prototype, not authorization to publish.
